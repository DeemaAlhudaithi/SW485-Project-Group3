Dataset Name: Diabetes Health Indicators Dataset
File: diabetes_binary_5050split_health_indicators_BRFSS2015.csv

Source (Kaggle):
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alexteboul/diabetes-health-indicators-dataset

Original Data Source:
CDC Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) 2015
https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/

License: CC0: Public Domain

Original Purpose:
The dataset was derived from the BRFSS 2015 health survey to study the
relationship between health/lifestyle indicators and diabetes, and to
support building predictive models for diabetes risk.

Description:
- Records: 70,692
- Predictor features: 21
- Target: Diabetes_binary (0 = No Diabetes, 1 = Prediabetes or Diabetes)
- Class balance: 50/50
